📖 Chemistry & Technical Architecture FAQ

How does Orbitalis compute molecular weights & percentages?
Orbitalis dynamically sums standard IUPAC atomic weights ($M_w = \sum n_i \times M_i$) for all atoms present in the active 3D coordinate model or formula. Each element's weight contribution and relative mass percentage is computed with up to 4 decimal precision and displayed alongside CPK color swatches.
What makes AMP & HTMX faster for molecular lookups?
Standard 3D chemistry applications load large Three.js bundles and WebGL shaders before showing data. The AMP and HTMX fast-edition renders core stoichiometric tables, properties, and IUPAC element references in sub-50 milliseconds with zero layout shift (CLS 0.00).
Which operating systems and devices are supported?
Orbitalis includes responsive vector icons and PWA configurations for Android (maskable squircle icons), iOS (Apple touch icons and WebApp status styling), macOS Safari (vector pinned tab masks), and Windows 10/11 (Modern Tile XML).
