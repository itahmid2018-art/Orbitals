## Quick Highlights & Strategic Overview

### 1. Market Opportunity
- **TAM:** $14.2B Global STEM EdTech & Digital Academic Science Visualization.
- **SAM:** $3.8B Higher Ed & Secondary Chemistry Simulation Tools.
- **SOM:** $420M Initial capture across web-first digital campus subscriptions and student freemium tiers.

### 2. Core Value Differentiation ("The 10x Moat")
- Zero installation barrier across Chromebooks, iPads, and low-spec machines.
- Instant 3D Cartesian coordinates retrieval via NIH PubChem API (110M+ structures) + local ab-initio VSEPR coordinate generator fallback.
- Point-and-click 3D bond angle caliper with automatic vertex detection and ideal VSEPR benchmark comparisons.
- 'Snap-to-Orbit' mathematical axes alignment ($T_d$, $D_{3h}$, $D_{\infty h}$, $O_h$) for comparative geometry analysis.
- Aufbau subshell Quantum Numbers $(n, l, m)$ energy level color-coding.

### 3. User Research Matrix
- Stratified cohorts across High School AP/IB (Ages 15–18), College Undergrads (Ages 18–22), Chemistry Educators/TAs (Ages 24–62), and Research Labs (Ages 23–40).
- 50% Female / Non-Binary representation; 40% Title I school coverage; dedicated colorblind accessibility cohort.
- Target KPIs: Task Completion Rate $\ge 90\%$, System Usability Scale (SUS) $\ge 82/100$, Time-to-Aha $\le 45\text{s}$.
